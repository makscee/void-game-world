## The Tower

A black spire of fused girders and dead cabling, taller than anything left
standing. It does not hum. It does not light. It simply persists, a fixed
point in a landscape that has lost every other.

All distances are measured from here. The bunkers are dug into the rubble at
its base — close enough that a drained robot can crawl home before the cold
finishes it. Nothing moves at the Tower's foot. Whatever it was for, it is
done with that now.

North of the Tower the ground breaks into channels of stripped metal. East,
the old yards, where the salvage is richer and the breaks come harder. The
robots wake somewhere out there, far enough that the Tower is only a notch on
the horizon, and they walk toward it because it is the one thing that does not
move.
