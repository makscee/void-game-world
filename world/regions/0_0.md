## The Tower

Sealed. No one knows what is inside.

A black spire of fused girders and dead cabling, taller than anything left
standing. The entrance, if there ever was one, is gone — the ground-level
plates are fused solid, the upper hatches corroded shut. It does not hum. It
does not light. It simply persists, a fixed point in a landscape that has lost
every other.

All distances are measured from here. The robots wake far out, far enough that
the Tower is only a notch on the horizon, and they walk toward it because it is
the one thing that does not move. The journey takes more than one charge.

Locked. Unknown contents. No unlock mechanic has been found.
