A broad concrete apron, cracked and heaved by frost and root. This was a truck staging ground — bay lines survive as faded paint ghosts, curbs split and scattered wide. A fuel pump island holds the centre: two heavy iron columns, nozzle ports packed with mineral scale, hoses long stripped away. Weeds occupy every joint and fault. The ground is level but shattered, carrying a faint mineral taint of old oil.
