A frost-heaved service apron of oil-stained concrete, cracked into broad irregular plates by decades of freeze-thaw. A loading canopy along the east edge has collapsed inward, its corrugated steel roof bowed flat against the rubble beneath it. Rusted dock hardware — cleats, a snapped bollard, a chain anchored to nothing — protrudes from the slab edges. The air carries old diesel and wet aggregate. No shelter. No movement.
