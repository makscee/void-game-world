Soft ground south of the clay flats — soil that holds moisture the upper plates have long shed. A collapsed wire fence bisects the tile east–west, its galvanised posts still upright at uneven intervals, the wire between them pressed flat by years of weight and neglect. Dead grass in matted brown sheets. The surface carries a faint damp sheen even without recent rain. No structures, no elevated ground, nothing to shelter behind or climb. The land here drains badly and remembers it.
