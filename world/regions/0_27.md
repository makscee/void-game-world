Foundation rubble at the edge of a former service yard. Broken cinder block and cracked slab pushed into low mounds by decades of frost heave. A cable spool lies half-buried, rust-fused to the ground. Perimeter fence posts remain upright but the chain-link is long gone — only the anchor-holes in the concrete record where it stood.
